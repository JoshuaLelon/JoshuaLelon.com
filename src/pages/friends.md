People I like and admire — some I know well, some I only know through their work online.

- [Taylor Pearson](https://taylorpearson.me) — His essay "Markets Are Eating the World" completely changed the way I think about everything; I love his writing and thought process.
- [Brooke Bowman](https://brooke.wtf/) — One of the kindest, most genuine, easiest-to-talk-to people I know. If I made a list of people who should rule the world, she'd be on it.
- [Valerie](https://valspals.substack.com/) — Incredibly smart but super humble, and so easy to just exist with. I'm so fortunate to have her in my life.
- [Nick Gray](https://nickgray.net) — Super smart, funny, and interesting guy. One of a few shining examples of people that, in my mind, have figured out who they are, figured out how to show the world who they are, and the world is so much better because of it.
- [Paul Millerd](https://pmillerd.com) — Incredibly thoughtful, and I admire his ability to be totally himself without caring what people think.
- [Michelle Varghese](https://michelleelisabethvarghese.com/) — Incredibly easy to talk to: smart, perceptive, humble, and funny.
- [Visakan Veerasamy](https://visakanv.com/) — Several of his ideas, tweets, and essays have changed my life, especially his tweet thread on agency.
