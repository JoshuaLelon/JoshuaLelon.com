## problem solved

i often want to keep the intent of a sentence while trying different ways of making its point. choosing a form such as metaphor or simile gives me a concrete way to direct the rewrite, with ai handling the wording. the replacement returns to its place in the essay so i can read it in context.

## interface inspirations

- [push pop press’s _our choice_](https://interfacelineage.com/push-pop-press-our-choice) lets readers pull graphics out of prose, interact with them, and return them to their original context. here, a sentence leaves the paragraph for a focused edit before its replacement returns.
- [imessage’s send transition](https://interfacelineage.com/imessage-send-bubble-transition) carries typed words from the input into a message bubble. here, carrying the sentence between layouts makes its relationship to the paragraph visible.

## interaction patterns

- [shared element transitions](https://docs.flutter.dev/ui/animations/hero-animations) carry an element between views by animating its position and size. here, the sentence moves from the paragraph into the comparison, and the replacement moves back.
- [object constancy](https://bost.ocks.org/mike/constancy/) lets someone visually track an element through a transition. here, the words stay recognizable as they move and reflow, so the reader can follow which passage is being edited.

## design decisions

- **make form the control.** the arrow connects _metaphor → simile_ because rhetorical form is what the writer changes. the simile label appears first; its bracket and sentence follow together as the generated result.
- **preserve the surrounding context.** the selected sentence starts inside a continuous paragraph. nearby prose dims while the sentence is annotated, then returns after the replacement settles into place.
- **separate watching from explanation.** the demo loops through the interaction. the walkthrough adds explanations in a reserved area below the text, letting the reader advance at their own pace.
